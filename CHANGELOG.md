# Changelog

## 0.3.1, 26 November 2026

- The website says who made it.
- On a laptop, an app named by version updates to the next version's name and keeps its shortcuts.
- The release workflow stays off until it is turned on, so it no longer reports failures.

## 0.3.0, 26 November 2026

- Connect from anywhere: on another Wi-Fi or mobile data, the phone reaches the laptop over the internet, direct when the networks allow.
- Update checks in both apps. Windows downloads, verifies and installs a new version when you click.
- The Windows window resizes, and shows everything on one screen when wide or maximised.
- Releases can be built and published by GitHub Actions from a tag.

## 0.2.1, 25 November 2026

- Both apps show their version in Settings.
- New website: smooth scrolling, its own pointer, a deck you can press, and a list of changes.
- One script now updates the version, downloads and checksums everywhere.

## 0.2.0, 25 November 2026

- First public release.
- Trackpad, keyboard, air mouse, slides, media, deck, gamepad, clipboard, files both ways and the laptop's screen.
- USB, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, switchable from any page.
